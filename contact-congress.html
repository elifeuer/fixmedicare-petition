Copy Your Message
Pre-written, nonpartisan, covers all 12 reforms
Dear Senator / Representative, My name is [YOUR NAME] and I am a constituent writing to demand your immediate support for meaningful Medicare and Social Security reform. Medicare and Social Security are not government handouts. They are earned benefits that Americans paid for over a lifetime of work. Congress has a moral and legal obligation to ensure they function as promised. I am asking you to take action on the following 12 critical failures: 1. LATE ENROLLMENT PENALTIES (Part B and D): Beneficiaries face permanent surcharges of 10% per year for Part B and 1% per month for Part D, often through no fault of their own. This punitive policy must end. 2. HOME CARE ACCESS: Medicare's home health benefit is severely restricted, forcing seniors who could live independently into costly institutional care. Expanded home care coverage is urgently needed. 3. HEARING AID COVERAGE: Medicare provides zero coverage for hearing aids despite hearing loss being one of the most prevalent senior conditions. Devices cost $3,000 to $7,000. This gap must be closed immediately. 4. MEDICAL SUPPLIES: Coverage for durable medical equipment is inconsistent and bureaucratic, leaving beneficiaries navigating complex prior authorization for basic items. 5. DENTAL COVERAGE: Original Medicare excludes all routine dental care. Poor oral health is directly linked to heart disease, diabetes, and malnutrition. This is a dangerous and inexcusable gap. 6. VISION COVERAGE: Routine eye exams and eyeglasses are not covered. Vision loss is the leading cause of falls. Falls are the leading cause of injury-related death in seniors. This gap costs lives. 7. PRICE TRANSPARENCY: Beneficiaries receive procedures with no prior disclosure of cost-sharing obligations, leading to medical debt and financial shock. Seniors deserve to know what care will cost. 8. LASER CATARACT SURGERY: Medicare covers traditional cataract surgery but excludes laser-assisted techniques, creating a two-tier system based on wealth. This inequity must end. 9. DRUG COSTS AND PART D: Unpredictable Part D premium increases and inconsistent formularies place essential medications out of reach for seniors who need them to survive. 10. PHYSICIAN REIMBURSEMENT: Low reimbursement rates are driving doctors to stop accepting Medicare patients, shrinking access to care for seniors across the country. 11. NON-EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION: Coverage for rides to medical appointments is limited to emergencies, leaving seniors without access to routine and specialist care they urgently need. 12. SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFIT ADEQUACY: Average benefits fall far short of covering housing, utilities, food, and medications as inflation outpaces Cost-of-Living Adjustments. Seniors cannot survive on what they receive. These failures affect 65 million plus Americans, including your own constituents. I respectfully demand that you champion reforms to address every one of these issues. Please visit www.fixmedicare.org to learn more and add your support to our national nonpartisan petition. Respectfully, [YOUR NAME] Constituent | www.fixmedicare.org
